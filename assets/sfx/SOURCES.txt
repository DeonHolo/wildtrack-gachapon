The turn cue uses an ElevenLabs Sound Effects library sample. ElevenLabs
describes this item as “Robotic arm clicking clunking” and its AI sound
effects page says free-plan use requires attribution to elevenlabs.io.
Source page: https://elevenlabs.io/sound-effects/ai
Original download: https://eleven-public-cdn.elevenlabs.io/payloadcms/2fzr3hildqd-ai-sfx-8.mp3
Bundled edit: trimmed to 1.25 seconds, faded out over the final 0.20 seconds,
and attenuated by 0.8 with ffmpeg/libmp3lame for a restrained toy mechanism.
The game plays the crank at 1.35× speed to follow the handle through one turn.
